# Image prompts — Phonon Characters

One landscape portrait per character. These prompts are written for ChatGPT / GPT Image,
but work in any image model that accepts a long descriptive prompt.

## How to use

1. Generate each prompt at **landscape 1504×1046**, or larger with the same ratio.
2. Keep the same camera height, lens, lighting and framing for all twelve.
3. Save as `Resources/Characters/<id>/portrait.png`.
4. The app renders the image full-bleed at its original aspect ratio. Keep the character's
   head and shoulders inside the frame with ambient space; do not add a hard frame or border.
5. The app draws its own aura and emblem badge, so leave the chest clear of badges, logos and lettering.
6. If the generator adds a watermark or text, crop it out or regenerate.
7. If the tool has a negative-prompt field, use the **Avoid** list at the bottom. If it does not,
   append the same list as a sentence beginning `Avoid:`.
8. If you want the app's paper stage to show through, add `flat chroma-green background
   (#00FF00)` to the prompt; the green is keyed out and the character keeps the full frame.
   Otherwise the painted background stays inside the image.

The app's visual world is warm paper, ink and storybook colour. The portraits should feel like
richly painted plates from an illustrated novel, not photographs and not flat vector art.

## Global style

Append this to every character prompt:

```text
Vibrant fantasy storybook portrait, richly saturated colour, fine ink linework with
watercolour and gouache shading, engraved-detail textures, warm parchment atmosphere,
luminous skin and fabric, dramatic soft key light with a subtle rim light, painterly
brushwork, high detail, centered bust, head and shoulders, face and eyes fully visible,
generous headroom, landscape composition, softly painted atmospheric background.
```
